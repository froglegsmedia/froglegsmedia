1. Offer plan

Recommended: The Cozy Season Sale + Doorbuster Weekend
Nov 2 to Nov 26: 15% off everything with code COZY15.
Nov 27 to Nov 29: 25% off everything with code DOORBUSTER.
All month: free gift wrap on purchases over $40.
Bonus: the first 25 Doorbuster shoppers get a free candle.

Why: a steady 15% keeps shoppers coming in early, and the jump to 25% gives them a reason to come back on Black Friday weekend. The free candle adds urgency without cutting your price further.
