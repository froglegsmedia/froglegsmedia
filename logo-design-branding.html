01What exactly is in the "brand book"?

A single reference document covering your logo and its variations, your color palette with exact codes, approved fonts, spacing and usage rules, and examples of it all applied. It's the file you hand to any designer, printer, or vendor so your brand stays consistent without you having to explain it every time.

02How many logo concepts do I see?

We present a focused set of real directions instead of a dozen throwaway sketches, then refine the one that's working rather than starting over from scratch each round.

03Do you design things beyond the logo?

Yes. Most projects include social templates, business cards, and a presentation deck built in the new system, so you leave with more than just a logo file.

04Can you rebrand a business that already has a logo?

Definitely. A lot of our branding work is a refresh: keeping what's recognizable, fixing what isn't working, and building the guidelines that never existed the first time around.
