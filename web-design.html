Frog-Quently
Asked.

01What platform do you build on?

Whatever actually fits the project, hand-coded for speed and full design control, or a platform like Squarespace, Shopify, or WordPress when you need to manage a store or update content yourself. We'll recommend one on the call.

02How long does a website take?

A focused marketing site typically takes a few weeks from kickoff to launch. Larger sites with e-commerce or lots of pages take longer, we'll give you a real timeline once we know the scope.

03Do you handle hosting and domains?

We'll set it up and point everything correctly at launch. You own the domain and hosting accounts, we don't lock your site behind an account you can't access.

04Can you redesign a site we already have?

Yes. A lot of our web work is replacing an outdated or DIY site with one that actually matches the brand and content we're already producing for a client.
