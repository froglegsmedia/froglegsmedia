How We Got Here

Started With a Camera.
Grew Into a Studio.

Frog Legs Media began the way most production companies do — with a camera, a laptop, and a founder (hi, Tyler) who couldn't stand watching good ideas get lost in translation between the agency, the production company, and whoever handled the account afterward.

Eight years and 50+ projects in, the video side of the business is stronger than ever. But the same problem kept showing up one level up: clients who loved the video work still had to go find a separate photographer, a separate composer, a separate social media manager, and a separate ads person — and brief every single one of them from scratch.

So we built out the rest. Same crew, same creative instincts, now covering photography, original music, social media management, and paid ad accounts — on top of the video production FLM was built on.
