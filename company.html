Started With a Camera.
Grew Into an Agency.

Frog Legs Media started with one person who loved making videos, not an agency. Coming from a music and film background instead of a marketing one, I saw content differently than most of what was flooding client feeds, and the work started standing out because of it.

As the jobs got bigger, so did the operations around them. I've sat through setups where two separate agencies needed sign-off on a couple of simple handheld shots on a beach: more meetings than shots, more approvals than ideas. All that noise wasn't making the work better. It was just burning time and budget that should've gone into the video itself.

So I built Frog Legs Media to cut out everything I couldn't stand about that world. One place I can trust, run efficiently, focused on results that actually move the needle instead of the process around them.

The name isn't random, either. Frog Legs comes from a song I wrote years back, the one people still ask me to play more than anything else. When it came time to name this thing, it just fit: personal, a little unexpected, impossible to forget. It's also the whole idea behind the work: come in a little unsure of yourself, and find your frog legs by the time we're done.
