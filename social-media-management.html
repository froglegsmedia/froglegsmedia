Things You're Probably

wondering.

What platforms do you actually post to?

Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook are most common — we'll scope the right platforms for your audience on the call.

How long is the commitment?

Retainers work best over a few months so we have room to actually build momentum — we'll walk through terms on the call rather than lock you into fine print you haven't seen.

Who actually shoots the content?

The same in-house crew behind our video and photo work — not a subcontractor you've never met.

Do I need to be in front of the camera?

Not if you don't want to be — plenty of brands run entirely on product, space, and behind-the-scenes content. We'll figure out what fits you on the shoot day.

Can I see content before it goes live?

Yes — the month's content calendar gets reviewed and approved upfront, so nothing goes live that you haven't seen.

What if I already have a brand kit?

Even better — send it over and we'll shoot and edit to match it, instead of starting from scratch.

How is this different from hiring in-house?

One hire gets you one person's time, no camera crew, and no editing team. This retainer is a strategist, a shooter, and an editor — without the salary, benefits, or training runway.

Do you also run paid ads?

Yes — it pairs directly with the Paid Ad Management retainer, and content from your shoot day can double as ad creative.
