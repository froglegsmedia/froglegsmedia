Good Questions

Frequently
Asked.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook are most common — we'll scope the right platforms for your audience on the call.

What's the minimum commitment?

Retainers work best over a few months so we have room to actually build momentum — we'll walk through terms on the call rather than lock you into fine print you haven't seen.

Where does the monthly shoot day happen?

Usually on-site at your business or location — we bring the full gear kit, you don't need to provide anything.

Do I need to approve posts before they go out?

Yes — the month's content calendar gets reviewed and approved upfront, so nothing goes live that you haven't seen.

Will it actually look like my brand?

Yes — it's the same crew handling your video and photo work, following whatever brand guidelines you already have.

How is this different from just hiring a social media manager?

One hire gets you one person's time, no camera crew, and no editing team. This retainer is a strategist, a shooter, and an editor — without the salary, benefits, or training runway.

What happens if I want more content than one shoot day covers?

That's the upgrade path in the retainer builder above — more shoot days, or a jump to full video production.

Can I add paid ads on top of this?

Yes — it pairs directly with the Paid Ad Management retainer, and the content from your shoot day can double as ad creative.
