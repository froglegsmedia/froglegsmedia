01Who actually qualifies for this program?

Early-stage startups, independent musicians and artists, and small businesses that don't yet have a production budget but have something worth telling. We're looking for good stories and good creative fit as much as need.

02What does "trade" actually mean here?

It depends on the project. Sometimes it's a reduced day rate with a small credit; sometimes it's a full trade, our production time for content plus brand visibility on the deliverables. We scope it case-by-case on the call.

03Will your logo be plastered all over my content?

No. Usually it's a small end card, a watermark, or a line in the caption, all agreed on before we shoot, never something that gets in the way of your brand.

04How do I apply?

Use the button below, tell us who you are and what you're building, and we'll follow up if it's a fit. Space in the program is limited, so we can't take every applicant, but every application gets a real look.
