Frog-Quently
Asked.

01Which platforms do you support?

TikTok Live and TikTok Shop, Whatnot, Instagram Live, Facebook Live Shopping, and YouTube Live. If you're selling somewhere else live, ask, and we'll tell you if we can support it.

02Do you provide a host, or just production?

Primarily production: cameras, switching, lighting, audio, and show flow. We can coach pacing and structure for your existing host, and can connect you with on-camera talent if you need one.

03Can you shoot at our warehouse or office instead of a studio?

Yes, most live selling shoots happen wherever the inventory already is. We bring a mobile production setup to you rather than making you haul product to us.

04What happens if something breaks mid-stream?

We run redundant gear and a backup connection on every live show, and someone is dedicated to watching stream health the entire time, not just running camera.
